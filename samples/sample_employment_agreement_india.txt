Section 1. Appointment and Position

1.1 The Company appoints the Employee as Senior Data Engineer, reporting to the Head of Engineering, with effect from 1 April 2026 (the "Joining Date").

1.2 The Employee shall devote their full working time and attention to the business of the Company and shall perform such duties as the Company may assign from time to time, including duties outside the Employee's stated role, at any location in India or abroad as the Company may direct.

Section 2. Compensation and Benefits

2.1 The Employee shall receive a fixed annual cost-to-company of INR 24,00,000 (Rupees Twenty-Four Lakh only), payable monthly in arrears, subject to applicable tax deductions.

2.2 The Employee may be eligible for an annual performance bonus of up to 15% of fixed pay, payable at the sole and absolute discretion of the Company. The Company may withhold, reduce or cancel any bonus without assigning reasons.

2.3 The Employee shall be entitled to statutory benefits including Provident Fund and Gratuity in accordance with applicable law.

Section 3. Probation and Termination

3.1 The Employee shall be on probation for a period of six (6) months from the Joining Date, which the Company may extend at its discretion.

3.2 The Company may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, without notice and without payment of any compensation in lieu of notice.

3.3 The Employee may resign only by giving the Company ninety (90) days' prior written notice. The Company may, at its sole discretion, refuse to accept a shorter notice period or a payment in lieu of notice, and may withhold the Employee's full and final settlement and relieving letter until the entire notice period is served.

Section 4. Confidentiality

4.1 The Employee shall keep strictly confidential, both during employment and at all times thereafter without limit of time, all information of any kind relating to the Company, its clients, products, finances, employees, technology and business plans ("Confidential Information").

4.2 The obligations in this Section shall apply regardless of whether such information is or becomes publicly available, is already known to the Employee, or is independently developed by the Employee.

4.3 The Company shall have no reciprocal confidentiality obligation towards the Employee.

Section 5. Intellectual Property

5.1 The Employee hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all rights, title and interest in any and all inventions, software, works of authorship, designs, ideas and improvements conceived or developed by the Employee during the term of employment, whether or not made during working hours, whether or not using Company resources, and whether or not related to the Company's business.

5.2 The Employee waives all moral rights in such works and shall not be entitled to any additional compensation or royalty for any intellectual property assigned under this Section.

Section 6. Non-Compete

6.1 For a period of two (2) years following the termination of employment for any reason, the Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, be employed by, consult for, or own any interest in any business engaged in data analytics, software development or information technology services anywhere in the world.

6.2 In the event of breach of this Section, the Employee shall pay the Company liquidated damages of INR 50,00,000 (Rupees Fifty Lakh only), which the parties agree is a genuine pre-estimate of loss and not a penalty.

Section 7. Non-Solicitation

7.1 For a period of eighteen (18) months following the termination of employment, the Employee shall not solicit, approach or accept business from any client or prospective client of the Company, whether or not the Employee had any dealings with such client.

7.2 For the same period, the Employee shall not solicit or hire any employee of the Company.

Section 8. Training Bond

8.1 The Company will provide the Employee with specialised training. If the Employee resigns or is terminated for any reason within twenty-four (24) months of the Joining Date, the Employee shall pay the Company INR 5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakh only) as reimbursement of training costs, irrespective of the actual cost of training provided.

Section 9. Indemnification

9.1 The Employee shall indemnify and hold harmless the Company, its directors, officers and affiliates from and against any and all losses, damages, claims, penalties, costs and expenses, including legal fees, arising out of or in connection with the Employee's performance of duties, any breach of this Agreement, or any act or omission of the Employee, without limit as to amount.

Section 10. Limitation of Liability

10.1 The Company's total aggregate liability to the Employee under or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed INR 10,000 (Rupees Ten Thousand only).

10.2 No equivalent limitation shall apply to the Employee's liability to the Company.

Section 11. Dispute Resolution

11.1 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator appointed solely by the Company, seated in Singapore, and conducted in English under the rules of the Singapore International Arbitration Centre.

11.2 All costs of the arbitration, including the arbitrator's fees and the Company's legal fees, shall be borne by the Employee, irrespective of the outcome.

11.3 Notwithstanding the above, the Company may at its option seek injunctive or other relief before any court of competent jurisdiction.
